# Top references

The construction, proportions and materials follow product photos of real Metal Fight tops
(used Takara Tomy tops photographed in parts, from beysandbricks.com listings for Counter Striker,
Dark Capricorn and Galaxy Capricorn): hexagonal face bolt with sticker, sculpted translucent clear
wheel, die-cast satin metal wheel with windows and steps, translucent track, small coloured tip.
The wheel shapes themselves are original, one per class (attack, defence, stamina, balance).

Sizes: metal wheel about 44-47 mm across and 8 mm deep, face bolt 11 mm across the flats,
tip to bolt about 28.5 mm. Rebuild and export: exec(open('art/tops/model.py').read()) in Blender,
then build(id) and export(id, repo) for each of fang, shell, gale, rook.
