# Launcher references

Modelled on Takara Tomy's Light Launcher (BB-34; the ripcord launcher in the Metal Fight starter sets),
from these product photos:

- Light Launcher with ripcord, face view: https://beysandbricks.com/light-launcher-version-1-beyblade-ripper-w-ripcord/
- BB-34 Light Launcher (orange) in its packet, back and side: https://beysandbricks.com/takara-tomy-light-launcher-1-metal-fusion-beyblade-ripper-w-ripcord-bb-34/

Sizes: housing about 49 x 40 x 15.5 mm; ripcord 6.8 mm wide, teeth every 2.2 mm, about 24 cm of
strip with a split ring handle 34 mm across. Exported to web/assets/launcher/launcher.glb.
